More than a browser

Nine things that make Hisn different from any other browser on your phone.

New

To any computer or iPhone

Open “Jisr → Any device” and scan the code on any computer or iPhone to send and receive files right in its browser. No app, no cable, and it resumes if the connection drops.

New

Shared clipboard

Copy text or a link on the computer and paste it on your phone, or the other way round, in one tap and without internet.

Blocks ads and trackers

Cleaner, faster pages, a blocker for harmful, adult, gambling and scam sites, and links stripped of tracking codes.

Password manager

Saves passwords encrypted on your phone and fills them with a tap, unlocked only by your fingerprint. With a strong-password generator and Chrome import.

A real media player

Video and music that keep playing in the background or in a floating window, with gestures for volume, brightness and seeking, plus picture and text viewers.

Automatic backup and sync

Like WhatsApp: everything is backed up to your own Google Drive automatically and comes back on a new phone, and your linked devices keep themselves in sync. All encrypted with your passphrase, readable by no one else.

Privacy and protection

It’s called a fortress for a reason. Protection is on from the first launch, and every option is yours to change.

  • Ad and tracker blocking

    Local block lists updated weekly, a count of what was blocked on each page, and one-tap exceptions per site.

  • Website blocker

    Block whole sites by category, such as adult, gambling, scam, phishing and malware, or add your own.

  • A private window that’s truly separate

    Runs in its own process with its own data folder, is wiped completely when closed, and never shows in screenshots.

  • Links without tracking

    Removes utm, fbclid, gclid and similar parameters before a page loads, and sends Global Privacy Control if you switch it on.

  • HTTPS only

    Tries a secure connection first and warns you before opening any unencrypted site.

  • Fingerprint lock

    Lock the app with your fingerprint or screen lock, block screenshots, and clear data automatically on exit.

  • Password manager

    Offers to save when you sign in and fills accounts with a tap. Passwords are encrypted with an Android Keystore key that only your fingerprint opens; import from Chrome with a CSV file.

  • Settings for each site

    Ads, JavaScript, blocking and clearing a site’s data, from one screen or right from the page menu.

Downloads without the wait

An advanced download manager written for Hisn, not the standard system one.

  • Up to 8 connections per file

    Splits the file and downloads the parts together, with pause and resume even after the app closes, a speed limit and a Wi‑Fi only option.

  • HLS and DASH video capture

    Detects streams as they play: pick the quality, audio and subtitles, and get a single MP4. DRM-protected content is never downloaded.

  • Save page

    Save any page as HTML, or as a full archive with images that opens without internet.

  • Search your downloads

    Find files by name or site, and filter by type or state: in progress, completed or failed.

Video and music, done right

A built-in player for all your files, and YouTube that keeps going when you leave.

  • Background playback

    Audio and video keep playing when you leave Hisn or lock the screen, with controls in the notification. Works in fullscreen too.

  • True fullscreen

    Wide videos turn sideways and fill the whole screen, camera area included, and no longer pause when you go fullscreen.

  • Hisn Player

    Playlists from your downloads, speed, subtitles and quality, a floating window, and it remembers where you stopped. Swipe for volume and brightness, double-tap to skip.

  • Pictures and text files

    A picture viewer with swipe, zoom and animated images, and a text and code viewer with line numbers that reads old Arabic files correctly.

  • Play streams from the page

    Any video Hisn finds on a page, even an m3u8 stream, plays straight in the player without downloading.

  • “Open with” from any app

    “Hisn Player” appears when you open a video, song, picture or text file from your file manager or gallery.

Your devices, together

Sharing and sync, without your data passing through anyone’s server.

  • Jisr: sharing without internet

    The receiver shows a code, the sender scans it, and its phone joins a private Wi-Fi made by Hisn to send files of any size in their original quality.

  • To any computer or iPhone

    In “Jisr → Any device” Hisn shows an address and a code. Open it in the browser of any device, Windows, Mac or iPhone, to send and receive files with no app, even without internet.

  • Shared clipboard

    Copy text or a link on the computer and paste it on the phone, or the other way round, in one tap.

  • Picks up where it stopped

    If the connection drops in the middle of a big file, Hisn reconnects and continues from the last piece that arrived instead of starting over.

  • End-to-end encrypted

    Every session gets fresh keys (ECDH and AES-256), and nearby devices show the same symbols so you know nobody is in between.

  • Share to Hisn from any app

    In your gallery or files choose “Share → Hisn Jisr”, then scan the other device’s code. The “Received” page opens your files in the player.

  • Automatic backup and sync

    An automatic backup of all your data in your own Google Drive, restored on any phone with your passphrase or recovery key. Linked devices keep themselves in sync: bookmarks, history, shortcuts, passwords and tabs.

Install in a minute

Hisn isn’t on Google Play yet, so it installs straight from the APK file.

  1. Download the file

    Tap the download button on your phone. Hisn.apk is saved to Downloads.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. If Android asks to allow installs from your browser or file manager, allow it once.

  3. Open Hisn

    Tap Install, then Open. You can make it your default browser in Settings.

Questions

Is Hisn free?

Yes, completely free, with no ads or subscriptions.

How do I update?

Inside Hisn: Settings → Updates & about. When a new version is out, a “Download update” button appears; install it over the current one and your bookmarks, history and settings stay as they are.

How does sharing without internet work?

Open Jisr on both devices. The receiver taps “Receive”, Hisn makes a private Wi-Fi and shows a code; the sender scans it, joins that Wi-Fi by itself and sends. Files travel directly and encrypted, with no internet or data plan.

Can I send to a computer or iPhone without Hisn?

Yes. Open “Jisr → Any device” and tap “Open for other devices”. If the other device is on the same Wi-Fi, open the address shown in its browser. If not, Hisn makes a private Wi-Fi the other device joins first (an iPhone can scan its code with the camera). Then you send and receive files and swap text from a page in the browser.

Is sync safe?

Yes. Hisn encrypts the backup and the sync file on your device with a passphrase you choose before saving them to your own Google Drive, so neither Google nor anyone else can read them. Hisn has no server at all.

What engine does Hisn use?

Pages are rendered by Android System WebView, which is based on Chromium and updates from your phone’s app store. That’s also why Chrome extensions aren’t supported.

Does the private window hide who I am?

No. It keeps nothing on your phone, but websites, your network and your internet provider can still see your activity. For anonymity you need a VPN or Tor.

Can it download any video?

It downloads direct files and unprotected streams (HLS and DASH). DRM-protected content and live streams are not downloaded, and Hisn tells you so.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Hisn has no server, never sends your history, bookmarks or downloads anywhere, and its data is excluded from cloud backup.
