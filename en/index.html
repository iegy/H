All video and download features

  • Up to 8 connections per file

Privacy

Privacy isn’t a hidden setting, it’s the foundation

No account, and no Hisn server at all. What’s yours stays on your phone, and what syncs is encrypted with your passphrase before it leaves.

Locked passwords and notes

Passwords are encrypted with a key in Android Keystore that only your fingerprint opens, and filled in with a tap. Notes have their own password, sync between your devices and are kept in the backup.

A private window that’s truly separate

Runs in its own process with its own data folder, is wiped completely when closed, and never shows in screenshots.

All privacy features

  • Links without tracking

    Removes parameters like utm, fbclid and gclid before opening links, and sends the Global Privacy Control signal if you turn it on.

  • HTTPS only

    Tries the secure connection first and warns you before opening any unencrypted site.

  • Fingerprint lock

    Lock the app with your fingerprint or screen lock, block screenshots, and clear data automatically on exit.

  • Per-site settings

    Ads, JavaScript, blocking and site data for any site, from one screen or straight from the page menu.

  • Separate profiles

    Work, study and fun, each with its own bookmarks, history, tabs and extensions, and different logins on the same sites.

  • Encrypted backup

    An automatic backup of all your data in your own Google Drive, encrypted with your passphrase so nobody else can read it.

Install in a minute

Hisn isn’t on Google Play yet, so it installs from an APK file, and after that it updates itself from inside the app.

  1. Download the file

    Tap the download button on your phone and Hisn.apk is saved to your downloads.

  2. Allow the install

    Open the file. If Android asks to allow installs from your browser or file manager, allow it once.

  3. Open Hisn

    Tap “Install”, then “Open”. You can make it your default browser in Settings.

Questions

Is Hisn free?

Yes, completely free, with no ads or subscriptions.

How do I update it?

Inside Hisn: Settings → Updates and about. When a new version is out, a “Download update” button appears; install it over the current one and your bookmarks, history and settings stay as they are.

Does it support Chrome extensions?

Hisn runs user scripts in the Tampermonkey format, which covers most of what people use extensions for: from the Hisn store or from Greasy Fork. Chrome Web Store extensions themselves (CRX files) are not supported, because Hisn uses Android System WebView.

Does the private window hide who I am?

No. It keeps anything from being saved on your device, but websites, your network and your internet provider still see your activity. To hide your identity you need a VPN or Tor.

Can it download any video?

It downloads direct files and unprotected streams (HLS and DASH). DRM-protected content and live streams are not downloaded, and Hisn tells you so clearly.

How does sharing work without internet?

Open “Jisr” on both devices. The receiver taps “Receive”, Hisn creates a private Wi‑Fi network and shows a code, and the sender scans it, joins automatically and sends. Files travel directly and encrypted.

Are sync and backup safe?

Yes. Hisn encrypts the backup and the sync file on your device with a passphrase you choose before they are saved to your own Google Drive, so neither Google nor anyone else can read them. There is no Hisn server at all.

Does the timer keep going if I close the app?

Yes. The Pomodoro timer sets a system alarm and shows a countdown notification and an alert when each phase ends, even with the screen locked.
